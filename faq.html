📚 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I setup the bot?

Invite the bot and run /setup to configure voucher logging.

Why are slash commands not showing?

It may take a few minutes for Discord to sync commands. Reinviting the bot can also help.

Why is voucher logging not working?

Make sure Critism's Voucher has all required permissions in your server.

Is Critism's Voucher free?

Yes. All core features are currently available for free.

Where can I get support?

Visit the Support page or join our Discord support server.
